⚓ About Me

I am Xiantao Hu, a Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Nanjing University of Science and Technology, working in the PCA Lab. I am supervised by Prof. Jian Yang and Associate Prof. Ying Tai.

My research focuses on visual object tracking and multimodal visual perception, including RGB–T tracking and 3D point cloud tracking. Previously, I received my master's and bachelor's degrees from Guangxi Normal University, where I was supervised by Prof. Bineng Zhong during my master's studies.
